Formulas, as on Sheet2: most items = (Total − Leftovers) × Price. Rice, beans and spaghetti are weighed in grams: Total = (Open − pot weight) + (Additions − pot weight), and Sales = (Total − Leftovers) ÷ portion size × Price (150 g, spaghetti 100 g). If Total is blank, Open + Additions is used. Grand sales = sum of all item sales.
